Questions

Do I need a udozo.com account?

Yes, the shop owner signs up with a Udozo seller account, which is free. Your staff don't need one; they get a PIN.

Can I sell items that aren't shown online?

Yes. Add them on udozo.com, tick "Sell at POS" and set them to shop only.

What equipment do I need?

Any phone, tablet or PC works. Bluetooth or USB receipt printers, barcode scanners and cash drawers are optional.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from Settings › Plan. Your udozo.com listings keep working as before.
