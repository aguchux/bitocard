Sell from any device

Use pos.udozo.com in a browser, or install the Udozo POS app. Everyone signs in the same way: owners with their Udozo account, staff with the shop code and their PIN.

Android

Phones, tablets and Android POS terminals. Works with Bluetooth receipt printers and barcode scanners. Get it on Google Play Download APK for POS terminals

iPhone & iPad

Turn an iPad into a counter till, or sell from an iPhone at markets and pop-ups. Download on the App Store iOS 16 or later

Windows

For counter PCs with USB printers, cash drawers and scanners. Keeps selling when the internet drops. Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11

Any browser

Nothing to install. Open pos.udozo.com on a laptop, Chromebook or tablet. Open pos.udozo.com
