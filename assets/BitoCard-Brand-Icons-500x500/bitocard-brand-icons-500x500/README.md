# BitoCard Brand Icons — 500×500 SVG Pack

This pack records 98 requested brand marks across gift cards, African telecom operators, API suppliers and payment networks. Only source-backed artwork is included; marks without a suitable online source are listed in `UNRESOLVED-BRANDS.csv` and their placeholder SVGs are omitted.

## Contents

- `svg/gift-cards/` — gift-card brand marks
- `svg/african-telecoms/` — telecom operator marks
- `svg/suppliers/` — supplier marks
- `svg/payment-networks/` — payment network marks
- `SOURCES.csv` — exact asset URLs, brand references, markets, and source/license notes
- `UNRESOLVED-BRANDS.csv` — requested logos with no suitable source found
- `SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md` — license and attribution notes for Simple Icons artwork

## Asset formats and sources

- Included SVGs use a 500×500 canvas and resolution-independent vector artwork.
- 37 marks use Simple Icons vector artwork.
- 42 additional marks were sourced online (22 direct SVG assets and 20 raster originals recreated as vector paths).
- 19 requested marks remain unresolved and are omitted rather than replaced with invented logo art.
- Refer to `SOURCES.csv` for each asset's exact source and rights note. Official-site assets retain their owners' rights; confirm source terms and brand guidelines before production.

## Scope and trademark note

This is a broad starter catalogue, not an exhaustive list of every gift card, telecom operator or supplier worldwide. Telecom operators are listed as reusable brand marks; actual service coverage varies by country and reseller agreement. Confirm each brand's current artwork, trademark rules, market availability and downstream resale rights before production. A collection license does not grant rights to third-party trademarks.
